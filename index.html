Julie Chytilová

I am an associate professor at the Institute of Economic Studies, Charles University, and a researcher at the Economics Institute of the Czech Academy of Sciences. I am a Research Fellow at CEPR and IZA.

The questions I am most curious about concern discrimination, the sources and consequences of conflict, poverty, how other-regarding preferences are formed, and time preferences and self-control.

To study these questions, my collaborators and I collect new data, mostly through field experiments and surveys, in settings such as Kenya, Uganda, Georgia, India, Colombia and Slovakia.
